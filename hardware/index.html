Jan 2025 – now

Hardware Designer & Researcher, Pazzi Lab

reThink64 · Bioengineering · Fremont, CA

  • Designed a multiplex diffusion-cell fixture in Onshape (with Claude over MCP) and printed it on an A1, so the lab could run up to 96 transdermal-delivery tests in parallel instead of buying a ~$500 commercial Franz cell for each.
  • Each printed unit uses about $1.56 of filament. The team evaluated it against commercial Franz cells, and the manuscript is being prepared for publication in Nature Biomedical Engineering (impact factor 26.3).
  • Worked with 3D printing manufacturers to recycle thousands of the lab's waste polypropylene pipette boxes into printer filament for structural parts.
