    A FEW BEARINGS

    Make yourself at home.

    Look around
    Drag to orbit the room. Scroll or pinch to move closer. Tap any numbered marker to visit an exhibit.

    Choose Walk. Use W A S D or the arrow keys to move, and drag to look. Hold Shift to move faster. On a phone, use the direction pad. Press E or tap the prompt near an exhibit.
