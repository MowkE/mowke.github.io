Jan 2025 – Jul 2026

Lead Researcher, Pazzi Lab

reThink64 · Bioengineering · Fremont, CA

  • Lipid-vesicle research across GUVs, MLVs and lipid nanoparticles, using fluorescence microscopy, ImageJ and dynamic light scattering.
  • MLV hydration study (senior project): ran the NaCl and CaCl₂ screens on DOPC vesicles, imaged by fluorescence microscopy and measured in ImageJ. Team poster: “High Yields of Multilamellar Vesicles using Monovalent Ionic Buffers.”
  • GUV formation: tested four of the group's ten film solvents (acetone, ethanol, isopropanol and chloroform) and their evaporation conditions.
  • Membrane rupture: carmofur-analog studies using GUV popping assays.
  • Transdermal delivery: ran delivery testing on a 3D-printed multiplex diffusion platform (up to 96 parallel trials); the manuscript is in preparation for Nature Biomedical Engineering (impact factor 26.3).
  • Lab software: built custom APIs and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers connecting Claude to the plate reader, HPLC, LC-MS, UV-Vis and NMR, plus a dashboard and Matplotlib analysis scripts.
  • Also: a skin-mimetic MLV platform for cosmetic delivery.
