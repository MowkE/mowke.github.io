Neurogenesis
in a dish.

Neurons (blue) are post-mitotic and fire action potentials down their axons. Neural progenitors (violet) run the cell cycle: they round up, line their chromosomes on a spindle, and split. Some daughters become new neurons and grow an arbor as you watch. The cytometer on the right reads this same culture.
